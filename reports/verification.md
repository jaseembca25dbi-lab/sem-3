# IPL 2008--2026: 7 Questions & Answers

## Question 1

**Which team has won the IPL trophy the most times from 2008 to 2026?**

**Answer:** Mumbai Indians (MI) and Chennai Super Kings (CSK) --- 5
titles each.

## Question 2

**Who has scored the most runs in IPL history from 2008 to 2026?**

**Answer:** Virat Kohli --- 9,336 runs.

## Question 3

**Who has taken the most wickets in IPL history from 2008 to 2026?**

**Answer:** Yuzvendra Chahal --- 233 wickets.

## Question 4

**Which player has scored the most centuries in IPL history?**

**Answer:** Virat Kohli --- 9 centuries.

## Question 5

**Which player has taken the most hat-tricks in IPL history?**

**Answer:** Amit Mishra --- 3 hat-tricks.

## Question 6

**Which team has won the most IPL matches from 2008 to 2026?**

**Answer:** Mumbai Indians (MI).

## Question 7

**Who has hit the most sixes in IPL history from 2008 to 2026?**

**Answer:** Chris Gayle --- 357 sixes.
